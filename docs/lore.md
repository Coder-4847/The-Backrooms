# Lore bible (original; keep notes consistent with this)

**Premise.** You are unnamed. You noclipped out of reality while filming. The camcorder is yours; the tapes and notes you find were left by **Group 7**.

**Group 7** (six people went in, the seventh seat stayed empty on every roster):
- **Marlow Vance**, quartermaster. Counts everything twice. Founded the Almond Outpost. Believes in rules and rations.
- **Ines Okoro**, radio operator. Listens for the hum's pattern; believes the Backrooms transmit.
- **Tobias Reed**, surveyor. Bitter, funny, left the group once and came back. Knows the corridors by feel.
- **Dr. Hale**, medic. Went missing in the Deep. (Later: Faceling.)
- **Pim**, youngest. Left drawings. Stopped writing in Act 1.
- **June Ashdown**, cartographer. Found that the levels repeat a pattern: the places are *drawn*, not built.

**The Architect** drew the Backrooms and built one exit. Wants a keeper for the door. The **hum** is its breathing; fluorescents beat in time with it.

**Rules of this place (as Group 7 learned them).**
1. Light holds things back. Darkness invites them.
2. What you look at, stops. What you don't, comes closer. (Stick)
3. Quiet is a kind of safety. Noise carries one room, sometimes two. Hounds count footsteps.
4. Almond water is real and it helps. Do not ask who makes it.
5. The camcorder light blinking red marks a place the Architect has not drawn teeth in. Rest there.

**Tone.** Short, observational, a little funny until it isn't. No exposition dumps. Dates are written as "Day N" because nobody trusts clocks.
